# TCCG Vendor Relationship Activation Plan

**Status:** Target / application plan — no relationship below is represented as active until TCCG receives written acceptance, account confirmation or executed commercial terms.

## Objectives

1. Establish reliable construction and HVAC supply channels.
2. Obtain project-specific commercial pricing with quote provenance.
3. Improve technical support, training and product availability.
4. Build government-capable procurement paths where useful.
5. Create a measurable vendor scorecard for quality, lead time, service and commercial performance.

## Relationship qualification packet

Prepare once and reuse with controlled updates:

- legal entity name and W-9;
- EIN / tax documentation where requested;
- business address and contacts;
- contractor licenses where relevant;
- certificate of insurance;
- trade/service profile;
- service geography;
- projected annual purchases by category;
- project pipeline without disclosing protected client information;
- credit application materials;
- bank/trade references if requested;
- government-market profile and verified registrations when relevant;
- cybersecurity/data-integration contact for API/CDE relationships.

## Vendor scorecard

Score quarterly or after each material purchase on a 100-point basis:

| Dimension | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Product/service quality | 20 |
| On-time delivery / lead-time reliability | 20 |
| Commercial competitiveness | 15 |
| Quote accuracy and scope clarity | 10 |
| Technical support | 10 |
| Warranty / issue resolution | 10 |
| Digital ordering / data quality | 5 |
| Government/compliance support | 5 |
| Training / enablement | 5 |

### Status rules

- **Strategic:** >= 85 and no unresolved critical issues.
- **Approved:** 70-84.
- **Conditional:** 55-69 or an active corrective action.
- **Restricted:** <55 or a material quality, ethics, sanctions, counterfeit, cyber or delivery failure.

## Quote provenance standard

Every estimate quote used for live pricing should capture:

- vendor and contact;
- quote/reference number;
- quote date and expiration;
- project;
- manufacturer/model;
- inclusions/exclusions;
- freight;
- tax status;
- lead time;
- payment terms;
- escalation assumptions;
- substitution status;
- attachment or controlled document reference.

No website price, AI-generated price or historic price should be treated as a live project quote without explicit estimator approval and a visible pricing basis.

## 30-day activation sequence

1. Create/update TCCG qualification packet.
2. Open Grainger business account and establish account representative path.
3. Contact local/regional Carrier and Trane commercial channels for account/dealer requirements.
4. Enroll or assess Johnson Controls Contractor Loyalty Program applicability.
5. Submit Schneider EcoXpert expression/application if controls/energy-management scope justifies certification investment.
6. Select Autodesk path: customer/subscription, service provider/referral, or integration partner based on TCCG/Tolani Labs commercial model.
7. Obtain written account/channel status and commercial contacts.
8. Load approved vendors and account evidence into the TCCG vendor registry.
9. Establish first live quote records and vendor scorecards.
