## Differentiators

### Digital construction operating system
TCCG integrates BIM information management, cost codes, estimating, procurement, QA/QC, HSE, owner reporting and closeout around common project data rather than disconnected spreadsheets.

### Tolani Labs workforce and R&D pipeline
Tolani Labs supports structured BIM, estimating, field-technology, QA/HSE and project-controls training, simulation and supervised internship pathways aligned to TCCG role demand.

### AI-assisted staff workflows with human control
TCCG is developing governed staff-agent prototypes for preconstruction, estimating, drawing comparison, RFIs, submittals, schedule risk, procurement, safety, QC, daily logs, changes, cost, closeout and lessons learned. Material project decisions remain under authorized human review.

### Evidence-first owner transparency
Owner dashboards are designed to expose schedule, cost, design, procurement, quality, safety and performance metrics with source and reporting-period context.

### Government-contracting readiness by design
TCCG maintains a controlled readiness register for SAM/UEI/CAGE verification, NAICS, certifications, bonding, insurance, labor/compliance review, cybersecurity requirements and solicitation compliance.

## Quality and safety approach

TCCG's quality model uses preparatory, initial, follow-up, deficiency/NCR and turnover gates with required evidence. The HSE model follows a proactive cycle of planning, pre-task hazard assessment, observations, corrective action, incident/near-miss learning and continuous improvement.

## Project delivery data

TCCG uses an internal cost-code and project-control taxonomy that preserves continuity from estimate through commitment, field cost, changes, forecast and closeout. The digital CDE baseline uses controlled WIP, Shared, Published and Archive information states with revision, approval, sensitivity and audit metadata.
