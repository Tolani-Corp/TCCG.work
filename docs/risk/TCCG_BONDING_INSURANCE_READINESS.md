# TCCG Bonding and Insurance Readiness Plan

**Status:** Underwriting-readiness and relationship-development plan. TCCG must not publish a bonding capacity or insurance limit until supported by a current broker/surety document.

## 1. Relationship architecture

TCCG should maintain two coordinated relationships:

1. **Construction insurance broker** — general liability, workers compensation, commercial auto, umbrella/excess, builder's risk/project-specific placement where needed, professional/technology E&O and cyber assessment.
2. **Construction surety agent / broker** — bid, performance, payment and ancillary bonds, capacity planning and SBA Surety Bond Guarantee access where appropriate.

The same agency may support both functions if it has sufficient construction expertise, but the surety relationship should be evaluated on its underwriting access and construction-bond experience rather than convenience alone.

## 2. Surety target market

The SBA publishes a current list of participating surety companies and an authorized agency directory. TCCG should request indications through qualified agents rather than representing any listed company as a TCCG relationship before underwriting acceptance.

## 3. Underwriting data room

Maintain current controlled copies of:

### Corporate
- articles/organization documents;
- ownership and affiliate structure;
- EIN/W-9;
- licenses;
- resumes of key construction personnel;
- organization chart;
- bank contact and credit/line information where applicable.

### Financial
- business financial statements;
- tax returns when requested;
- personal financial information/indemnity information only through approved secure underwriting channels;
- accounts receivable aging;
- accounts payable aging;
- cash and working-capital position;
- debt schedule;
- work-in-progress schedule;
- backlog and pipeline distinction;
- job-cost reports and estimate-to-actual history.

### Operational
- project list with role/value/duration/status;
- subcontractor-management approach;
- estimating controls;
- change-management process;
- safety program;
- quality-control program;
- claims/default/litigation disclosure where applicable;
- insurance loss runs when requested.

## 4. Bond-capacity model

Track three separate numbers:

- **single-job limit** — maximum supported bonded contract size;
- **aggregate program** — total bonded work supported at one time;
- **available capacity** — aggregate less current bonded backlog and underwriting adjustments.

Do not derive these numbers internally for proposal use. The operational dashboard may model scenarios, but the external figure must come from a current written surety indication or bond commitment.

## 5. SBA Surety Bond Guarantee path

SBA guarantees certain bid, performance, payment and ancillary surety bonds issued by participating sureties for eligible small businesses. SBA's current program page states eligibility can extend to contracts up to $9 million for non-federal work and $14 million for federal work, subject to size and underwriting requirements. The current SBA guarantee fee on performance/payment bond guarantees is 0.6% of contract price; bid bond guarantees have no SBA fee.

The program flow is:

`TCCG -> SBA-authorized surety agent -> participating surety -> SBA guarantee (when approved)`

Use the SBA agency directory to identify agents that operate in TCCG's target states. For eligible smaller contracts, ask the agent whether the Quick Bond Guarantee pathway is appropriate.

## 6. Insurance schedule baseline

The exact limits are contract- and jurisdiction-dependent. Build the broker review around:

| Coverage | Purpose / review point |
|---|---|
| Commercial general liability | bodily injury/property damage and contractual requirements |
| Workers compensation / employers liability | statutory and contract requirements |
| Commercial auto | owned, hired and non-owned vehicle exposure |
| Umbrella / excess | owner/prime excess-limit requirements |
| Builder's risk | determine whether owner, GC or TCCG procures on each project |
| Contractor's equipment / inland marine | tools/equipment and mobile property |
| Professional liability / E&O | BIM, design-assist, controls, consulting and professional-service exposure |
| Cyber liability | CDE, portals, project data, connected-building and privacy exposure |
| Pollution / contractors pollution | evaluate for HVAC refrigerants, environmental/remediation or contract requirements |

## 7. Certificate and subcontractor controls

TCCG should configure one certificate standard per project containing:

- required coverage and limits;
- additional insured language where contractually required;
- primary/noncontributory status where required;
- waiver of subrogation where required;
- completed-operations duration where required;
- automobile requirements;
- umbrella requirements;
- notice/cancellation requirements subject to available endorsements;
- expiration tracking;
- subcontractor flow-down.

No subcontractor should be marked insurance-qualified merely because a COI exists. Validate policy dates, carriers, limits and required endorsements against the subcontract.

## 8. Relationship selection scorecard

Score potential brokers/agents on:

- construction specialization — 20%;
- access to appropriate carriers/sureties — 20%;
- small/emerging contractor underwriting experience — 15%;
- SBA SBG capability — 15%;
- contract and certificate support — 10%;
- claims advocacy — 10%;
- responsiveness/service team — 5%;
- digital certificate/data capability — 5%.

## 9. First relationship meeting agenda

1. TCCG legal/ownership structure.
2. Current licenses and target geographies.
3. Service mix: GC, HVAC, BIM/VDC, controls and digital delivery.
4. Largest completed relevant projects by correctly attributed entity/person.
5. Current backlog versus pipeline.
6. Desired single/aggregate bonding targets.
7. Government-construction strategy.
8. Cash/working-capital and banking support.
9. Subcontractor strategy.
10. Insurance schedule and technology/professional exposures.
11. Underwriting deficiencies and 90-day improvement plan.

## 10. Activation evidence

Mark the relationship **active** only when the registry contains:

- broker/agency legal name and primary contact;
- executed broker-of-record or account relationship where applicable;
- current insurance policies/COIs;
- written surety capacity indication or bond commitment where applicable;
- renewal dates;
- secure data-room location;
- owner for renewal/underwriting updates.
